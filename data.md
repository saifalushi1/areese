# A different beginning

The live experience is authored in `src/App.tsx`. This file records its editorial basis; the previous slideshow is no longer the entry point.

## Main argument

Celebrate marriage simply and compare the opportunity cost of a large wedding with travelling together, building a home fund, and supporting meals for orphaned children. A wedding is not inherently haram, and expense alone does not establish a religious ruling. The concern is waste, interest-based borrowing, prohibited practices, and pressure to impress.

## Religious sources

- A walimah is encouraged: Sahih al-Bukhari 5167, https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5167
- Avoid wastefulness: Quran 17:26-27, https://quran.com/17/26-27
- Riba is prohibited: Quran 2:275, https://quran.com/2/275
- Avoid intoxicants: Quran 5:90, https://quran.com/5/90

Sources are linked beside the relevant statements in the experience. Entertainment and etiquette have differing scholarly interpretations. Specific plans should be discussed with a qualified scholar.

## Illustrative numbers

These are planning assumptions, not market averages, charity quotes, or financial forecasts.

- Starting wedding budget: USD 35,000; simple celebration: USD 3,000; savings: USD 32,000.
- Overseas trip for two: USD 6,000. Complete trips are rounded down and the remainder is shown.
- Home savings goal: USD 60,000. The displayed percentage is rounded to the nearest whole percent.
- Orphan meals: USD 3 each by default, adjustable between USD 1 and USD 100. Meals are rounded down. Check programme costs and delivery charges with the chosen charity.
- Each alternative separately uses the entire savings amount. Alternatives are not additive.
- Combined example: 25% travel, 60% home, 15% giving. Each dollar allocation is rounded down; the remainder stays in savings.
- A celebration allowance above the selected budget results in zero savings, with a visible explanation.
